      Chemistry

      Correlative EDS with the SE volume, and ways to map chemistry with a fraction of the EDS time: context-driven sparse EDS (deep kernel learning, after Kalinin and Ziatdinov) and coupled-NMF pan-sharpening with BSE (after Borodinov et al., 2019).

      Reconstruction quality by method

      What the benchmark shows

      • Sparse EDS guided only by the SE image misses chemistry that SE cannot see.
      • Adding the BSE image, whose signal follows composition, recovers most of it at a tenth of the EDS time.
      • Pan-sharpening with BSE also finds the hidden region; with few element channels it is limited by EDS counting statistics.

      EDS in this benchmark is simulated on the real segmentation. Run it with python examples/chemical_fusion.py.

      Correlate real EDS maps

      Import element-map exports (e.g. AZtec TIFFs), register them to the SE stack, find chemical phases and the composition of every segmented phase or particle.

      Acquire

      Follow an acquisition while the FIB mills, and run the self-driving agent on a digital twin of this sample before any beam time.

      Self-driving run on the digital twin

      The agent mills, refocuses, corrects drift, adapts dwell and slice thickness, takes sparse EDS and stops at the precision you ask for. Its Thermo Fisher AutoScript calls are recorded in dry-run only.

      Live acquisition

      Start it next to the microscope with nuscope-tomo watch <acquisition folder> -p <project> --voxel 20 5 5 --model rf.pkl. Studio follows the growing volume in the View workspace.
