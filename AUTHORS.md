# Authors

**NuScope-TOMO** was created by **Roberto dos Reis ([@rmsreis](https://github.com/rmsreis))**,
NUANCE Center, Northwestern University, who conceived the project, defined its scope
(an open, modern alternative to the Amira FIB-SEM workflow with correlative chemical
analysis) and directs its development.

Contributions are welcome via pull requests to
[NUANCE-IT/NUScope-TOMO](https://github.com/NUANCE-IT/NUScope-TOMO).
